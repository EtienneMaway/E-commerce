# Privacy Policy for KMB-Talk

**Last updated: 27 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the KMB-Talk mobile application ("KMB-Talk", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information. KMB-Talk is a business management tool that helps small traders and resellers manage inventory, sales, credit/debt, and financial reconciliation.

By creating an account and using KMB-Talk, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Who we are

KMB-Talk is operated by the developer of KMB-Talk ("the operator").

- **Contact email:** support@kmb-talk.com
- **Website:** https://kmb-talk.com
- **App package name:** com.kmb.mobile

---

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the app's features.

### a) Information you provide
- **Account information:** your name, email address, and phone number, used to create and secure your account and to sign you in.
- **Business and financial data you enter:** product/inventory records, purchase and selling prices, sales transactions, supplier and debtor records, payments, debts/credits, expenses, withdrawals, exchange rates, and related notes. This data is what the app exists to manage on your behalf.
- **Employee/employer relationships:** if you use the app as an employee acting on an employer's books (or as an employer), we store the link between those accounts to apply the correct permissions.

### b) Information collected automatically
- **Authentication tokens:** a secure login token is stored on your device (in the device's secure storage) so you stay signed in.
- **Push notification identifier:** if you enable notifications, we collect a push token from the device's notification service so we can send you alerts (for example, low-stock reminders).
- **App preferences:** your language (English/French) and theme (light/dark) settings, stored to personalize your experience.

### c) Information we do **not** collect
- We do **not** collect your precise location.
- We do **not** collect contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising or sell your data to advertisers.
- **Bluetooth** is used **only** to connect to a thermal receipt printer you choose to pair. We do not collect or transmit any data through Bluetooth; it is used locally on your device to print receipts.

---

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create, authenticate, and secure your account.
- Provide the core features of the app: inventory, sales, credit/debt tracking, payments, expenses, and financial reporting.
- Sync your business data so it is available across your sessions and devices.
- Send you operational notifications you have enabled (e.g. low-stock alerts).
- Maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the service.

We process your data to provide the service you requested and to operate and improve the app. We do **not** use your business or financial data for advertising.

---

## 4. How your data is stored and protected

- Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our servers.
- Account passwords are stored only in **hashed** form and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.
- Login tokens are kept in your device's **secure storage**.
- Your business data is stored on our backend infrastructure hosted with our hosting provider (Contabo). 
We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

---

## 5. Sharing of information

We do **not** sell your personal or business data. We share information only:
- **With service providers** that host and operate the app's infrastructure (e.g. our cloud hosting provider), strictly to run the service.
- **Between linked employer and employee accounts**, only to the extent needed to operate shared books, according to the permissions you set.
- **When required by law**, such as to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or valid government request.

---

## 6. Data retention

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active so the app remains useful to you. If you request account deletion, your personal data and associated business records are scheduled for deletion. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

---

## 7. Children's privacy

KMB-Talk is a business tool intended for adults (18+) and is **not directed at children**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

---

## 8. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate data (most data is editable directly in the app).
- **Delete** your account and associated data. The app provides an account-deletion option, and you can also request deletion by emailing us.
- **Withdraw consent** for notifications by disabling them in your device or app settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email in Section 1. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

---

## 9. International users

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence. By using the app, you consent to the transfer and processing of your information in those locations, with the protections described in this policy.
